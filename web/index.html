Mail, calendars, and contacts in one quiet, fast desktop app. Native where it counts, private by default, and built for people who live in their inbox.

Why Pavillion

JMAP first

Modern mail sync that is quick to open and quick to catch up, with a local store you can search instantly.

Small and native

A C core and a system web view. No bundled browser, no telemetry, no in-app updater.

Safe by design

Messages are sanitized before they render. Nothing runs, nothing phones home.

Everywhere you work

Linux and Windows today, macOS next, iPad after that.
